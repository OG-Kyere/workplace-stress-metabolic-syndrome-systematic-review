Domain totals:

- Physical activity: 9
- Multicomponent/occupational lifestyle: 13

Design totals:
- Randomized controlled trials: 7
- Cohort/longitudinal studies: 6
- Analytical cross-sectional studies: 18
- Quasi-/nonrandomized intervention studies: 3

This figure does not depend on unresolved PRISMA record counts and can therefore be used before the PRISMA flow diagram is finalized.
