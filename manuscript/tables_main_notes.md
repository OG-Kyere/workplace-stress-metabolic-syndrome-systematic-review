# Main manuscript tables

## Table 1 — Study characteristics

This table contains all 34 studies in the active pool and summarizes occupational population, study design, and main exposure/intervention. The definitive included-study count remains provisional until PRISMA screening counts are frozen.

## Table 2 — Principal findings

The main-paper findings table intentionally emphasizes studies with verified quantitative estimates or particularly informative longitudinal/intervention findings. Detailed results for every included study should remain in supplementary extraction tables.

## Table 3 — JBI appraisal summary

The main-paper JBI table summarizes appraisal patterns by design instead of reproducing every checklist item. Detailed item-level judgments belong in Supplementary Material.

Current design counts are generated from the corrected master table. Newly identified studies that have not yet undergone full item-level JBI appraisal are explicitly labelled pending rather than being assigned inferred quality ratings.
