# OEM reporting addendum

Target journal: **Occupational and Environmental Medicine (BMJ)**

This addendum contains reporting statements that should be inserted into the final OEM submission copy after the PRISMA counts are frozen. It does not alter the frozen scientific master.

## Registration and protocol

### Registration
This review update was not prospectively registered.

### Protocol
No prospectively registered protocol was available for the updated review. The revised eligibility framework, search strategy, screening rules, extraction structure, appraisal workflow and subsequent methodological decisions are archived in the associated project repository.

### Suggested Methods wording

> This review update was not prospectively registered. No prospectively registered protocol was available; however, the revised eligibility criteria, search framework, screening rules, extraction structure and appraisal workflow were defined before the final evidence synthesis and are archived in the project repository.

## Reporting-bias assessment

No formal publication-bias analysis was undertaken because the evidence was synthesised narratively and the included studies were heterogeneous in design, population, exposure or intervention, and outcome reporting.

### Suggested Methods wording

> Formal assessment of publication bias was not undertaken because no pooled meta-analysis was performed and the evidence was highly heterogeneous across study designs, occupational populations, exposures, interventions and outcomes.

## Certainty of evidence

No formal GRADE certainty framework was applied.

### Suggested Methods wording

> A formal GRADE certainty-of-evidence framework was not applied. Confidence in the findings was interpreted in relation to study design, directness, consistency across studies and design-specific Joanna Briggs Institute appraisal findings.

## Suggested Discussion limitation

> The review was not prospectively registered and no formal GRADE or publication-bias analysis was undertaken. These limitations should be considered alongside the substantial heterogeneity in occupational setting, exposure measurement, intervention design and outcome reporting. To support transparency, the eligibility framework, search strategy, extraction files and appraisal workflow are archived in the associated project repository.

## Statistical presentation for OEM

OEM currently advises:
- report confidence intervals for measures of association;
- use quantitative P values where they are needed rather than threshold-only reporting, when the source provides them;
- avoid unnecessary numerical precision;
- follow PRISMA for systematic reviews.

The scientific master currently retains some threshold-only P values where that is all the accessible source reported. These should not be replaced by invented exact values.

## Peer review

OEM currently uses **single-anonymised peer review**. Author identity therefore does not need to be removed from the final OEM submission copy.
