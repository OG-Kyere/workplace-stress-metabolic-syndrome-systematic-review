\section*{Supplementary Methods S1. Search strategy}

The updated review used PubMed/MEDLINE as the primary bibliographic source and covered 2015--2026. The core search combined concepts for metabolic syndrome, employed/workplace populations, and work stress, physical activity, sedentary behaviour, diet, nutrition, and workplace lifestyle intervention.

\begin{verbatim}
("metabolic syndrome"[Title/Abstract] OR "Metabolic Syndrome"[MeSH Terms])
AND
(worker*[Title/Abstract] OR employee*[Title/Abstract] OR workplace[Title/Abstract]
 OR occupation*[Title/Abstract] OR "shift work"[Title/Abstract]
 OR "white-collar"[Title/Abstract] OR "blue-collar"[Title/Abstract]
 OR police[Title/Abstract] OR driver*[Title/Abstract]
 OR healthcare[Title/Abstract])
AND
(stress[Title/Abstract] OR "job strain"[Title/Abstract]
 OR "physical activity"[Title/Abstract] OR exercise[Title/Abstract]
 OR sedentary[Title/Abstract] OR diet*[Title/Abstract]
 OR nutrition*[Title/Abstract])
AND
("2015"[Date - Publication] : "2026"[Date - Publication])
\end{verbatim}

Supplementary searches used narrower combinations for occupational stress, physical activity/exercise/sedentary behaviour, diet/nutrition/worksite food, shift work, and workplace interventions. Reference-list screening, citation chasing, publisher records, PubMed related-article searching, and secondary-review reference mining were also used.

Final PRISMA identification and screening counts remain to be inserted only after the complete record library has been exported and deduplicated.

\section*{Supplementary Note S1. Overlapping cohorts and companion reports}

The 2020 van Zon et al. and 2021 Runge et al. reports derive from the Dutch Lifelines workforce cohort and are therefore treated as overlapping reports rather than independent cohorts when participant totals or evidence independence are considered.

Protocol, feasibility, or secondary reports linked to completed workplace trials are treated as companion publications unless they contribute unique prespecified outcomes relevant to this review.
