## Figure 1. Evidence map of workplace metabolic-syndrome studies

A four-by-four evidence map displays the number of studies by primary evidence domain and study design. Rows represent occupational stress, diet/nutrition, physical activity, and multicomponent interventions; columns represent randomized controlled trials, cohort studies, cross-sectional studies, and quasi-experimental studies. Physical activity and multicomponent evidence are the most densely represented domains, while occupational-stress studies are concentrated in cohort and cross-sectional designs.
