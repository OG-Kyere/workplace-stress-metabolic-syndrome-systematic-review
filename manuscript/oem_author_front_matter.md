## Title
**Workplace Determinants of Metabolic Syndrome in Working Adults: Systematic Review**

## Article category
**Systematic review and meta-analysis**

Narrative synthesis was used because heterogeneity in occupational populations, exposures, interventions, MetS definitions and effect measures precluded a meaningful overall meta-analysis.
