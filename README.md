# Workplace Stress, Dietary Choices, Physical Activity, and Metabolic Syndrome

Systematic review examining the role of workplace stress, dietary choices, and physical activity in shaping metabolic syndrome and related metabolic risk among working adults.

## Project status

This repository currently contains the source materials for an undergraduate systematic review completed at the Department of Statistics and Actuarial Science, Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST), Ghana.

The work is being prepared for:
- conversion from thesis format to a journal-ready systematic review;
- public preprint dissemination; and
- subsequent journal submission.

## Review overview

- **Review period:** 2015–2025
- **Databases searched:** PubMed/MEDLINE, Scopus, and ScienceDirect
- **Records identified:** 1,214
- **Studies included:** 27
- **Framework:** PRISMA
- **Core exposures:** workplace stress, dietary choices, and physical activity
- **Outcome domain:** metabolic syndrome and related metabolic risk factors

## Reproducibility note

The current thesis source references an `abbreviations.tex` file that was not present in the archived source package. The original files will therefore be preserved as supplied, and the missing component will be repaired explicitly during manuscript preparation rather than silently reconstructed.

## Planned manuscript
