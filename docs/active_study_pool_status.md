# Active Study Pool Status

## Current working pool

The fresh workplace-focused search and subsequent reference/effect-extraction sweeps have identified **34 primary studies** that currently meet the working eligibility rule.

This supersedes earlier repository notes that referred to 28 studies as a complete set.

## Important

The number **34 is still a working evidence pool, not a final PRISMA inclusion count**.

The search remains open until:
1. the formal database strategy is run to completion;
2. reference-list and citation chasing are complete;
3. records are deduplicated;
4. all full-text eligibility decisions are frozen;
5. companion reports are linked to their parent studies.

## Why the pool changed

Six eligible workplace studies were identified during effect extraction:
- Kim et al. (2015) Internet-based lifestyle controlled trial;
- Choi et al. (2017) T'ai Chi randomized trial;
- Taiwanese workers physical-activity study (2017);
- Tsai et al. (2018) workplace physical-activity randomized trial;
- Gerber et al. (2020) prospective police study;
- remote physical-activity randomized trial (2024).

## Current methodological principle

Repository files should use the term **active study pool** until the formal search is closed. Earlier references to a 'locked final included set' should be interpreted as superseded.
