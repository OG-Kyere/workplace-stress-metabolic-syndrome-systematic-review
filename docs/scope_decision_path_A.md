# Scope Decision — Path A

## Decision

The journal/preprint version will retain a **strict workplace-focused review question**.

### Working review question

Among working adults, how are workplace stress, dietary factors, and physical activity associated with metabolic syndrome or its established metabolic components?

## Operational eligibility criteria

A study must satisfy all of the following:

1. **Population/context:** employed or working adults, or an explicitly occupational/workplace population.
2. **Exposure/intervention:** workplace/occupational stress, dietary factors, physical activity/sedentary behaviour, or closely related workplace lifestyle determinants.
3. **Outcome:** metabolic syndrome as a defined syndrome, incidence/prevalence of metabolic syndrome, or established metabolic-syndrome components measured as cardiometabolic outcomes.
4. **Study type:** completed original empirical research.
5. **Publication:** peer-reviewed, English language, 2015 onward, and available by the review's documented search end date.

### Automatic exclusions

- study protocols;
- editorials, conference abstracts, and grey literature;
- populations not tied to employment/workplace context;
- workplace studies reporting only stress/physical activity without metabolic outcomes;
- purely clinical/community studies with no occupational context.

## Important consequence

The original 27-study thesis set should **not** be carried forward unchanged. The journal manuscript will use a re-screened study set derived from the original candidates.

The archival thesis remains unchanged in `thesis_source/`.
