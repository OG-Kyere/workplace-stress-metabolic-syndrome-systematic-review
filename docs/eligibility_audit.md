# Preliminary Study-by-Study Eligibility Audit

This is a **screening audit**, not a final exclusion list. It compares the 27 studies listed in the thesis appendix with the eligibility language reported in Chapter 3.

## Working interpretation used for this audit

A study would be clearly in scope only if it simultaneously satisfied:

1. a working/employed adult population or explicit workplace/occupational setting;
2. workplace stress, diet, and/or physical activity as an exposure/intervention;
3. metabolic syndrome or clearly defined metabolic/cardiometabolic outcomes;
4. original completed research (not a protocol);
5. publication between 2015 and the reported search date.

## Additional verified issues

### Normandin et al. (2017)
The paper enrolled adults aged 65–79 years, which conflicts with the thesis population criterion of generally 18–65 years. The DOI recorded in the thesis seed file also appears incorrect; the article is indexed under **10.1249/MSS.0000000000001122**.

### Botoseneanu et al. (2017)
Participants were 70–89 years old and community-dwelling, making the paper incompatible with a strict working-age/workplace population criterion.

### Kiwata et al. (2017)
The publication is a **protocol/pre-results** paper. This conflicts with the thesis statement that study protocols were excluded.

### Zhao et al. (2025)
The publication title explicitly identifies it as a protocol. This also conflicts with the stated protocol exclusion.

### Jovanović et al. (2020)
The DOI recorded in the thesis appendix appears incorrect. PubMed indexes the article as **10.2478/aiht-2020-71-3379**.

### Andersen et al. (2021)
The DOI recorded in the thesis appendix appears incorrect. PubMed indexes the article as **10.1186/s12966-021-01237-1**.

### Suárez-Cuenca et al. (2025)
The DOI/title details in the thesis appendix appear inconsistent with the indexed article. PubMed lists the article as **Nutrients 2025;17(2):256**, DOI **10.3390/nu17020256**.

## Core methodological decision required

The current 27-study set cannot be defended under a strict interpretation of the thesis title and reported workplace eligibility criteria.

Before journal submission, the review must choose one of two defensible paths:

### Path A — Keep the workplace-focused question
Retain a title/question focused on working adults and workplace exposures, then re-screen the evidence and exclude non-workplace populations. This may substantially reduce the included-study count.

### Path B — Broaden the review question
Broaden the population/context to adults with metabolic syndrome or cardiometabolic risk, regardless of employment setting. This would preserve more of the 27 studies but would require a new title, objectives, eligibility criteria, and interpretation.

The archival thesis remains unchanged. No study should be permanently removed until the original screening records and full texts have been checked.
