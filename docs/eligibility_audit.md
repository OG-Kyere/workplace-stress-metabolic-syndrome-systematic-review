# Eligibility Audit

This file records issues that must be checked against the original full-text screening records before the journal/preprint manuscript is finalized.

## High-priority checks

### 1. Study-protocol conflict
The included-study appendix lists:

**Zhao et al. (2025)** — *At-home immersive virtual reality exergames to reduce cardiometabolic risk among office workers: Protocol for a randomized controlled trial.*

Chapter 3 states that study protocols were excluded.

**Action:** verify whether this record was mistakenly included, whether a completed trial publication was intended instead, or whether the eligibility criteria need correction.

### 2. Study-design conflict
Chapter 3 gives two incompatible design rules:
- qualitative, quantitative, or mixed-method studies; versus
- randomized controlled trials only.

The included list contains several clearly non-randomized designs.

**Action:** recover the actual screening rule used and rewrite the Methods accordingly.

### 3. Population/context fit
Some included studies may involve participants with metabolic syndrome or related conditions but are not obviously workplace-based from the title alone.

**Action:** verify, study by study, whether employment/workplace context was required, optional, or merely part of the review framing.

### 4. Review outcome definition
The thesis alternates between:
- metabolic syndrome as a syndrome-level outcome; and
- individual metabolic components/risk factors.

**Action:** define the eligible outcome domain explicitly for the journal manuscript.
