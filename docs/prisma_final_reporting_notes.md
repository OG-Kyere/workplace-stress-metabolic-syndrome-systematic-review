# PRISMA reporting notes to add at finalisation

These statements should be added to the final manuscript or supplement before submission.

## Registration
Suggested wording:

> This review update was not prospectively registered. The eligibility criteria, search framework, screening decisions, extraction files and appraisal workflow are documented in the associated project repository.

## Reporting-bias assessment
Suggested wording:

> Formal assessment of publication bias was not undertaken because the evidence was synthesised narratively and the included studies were heterogeneous in design, population, exposure, intervention and outcome reporting.

## Certainty assessment
Suggested wording:

> A formal GRADE certainty-of-evidence framework was not applied. Confidence in the findings was interpreted in relation to study design, directness, consistency and design-specific JBI appraisal findings.

## Protocol
Suggested wording:

> No prospectively registered protocol was available for the updated review. The revised eligibility framework and search strategy were defined before the final evidence synthesis and are archived in the project repository.

These statements should be inserted only in the final target-journal submission copy, not retrofitted into the historical thesis archive.
