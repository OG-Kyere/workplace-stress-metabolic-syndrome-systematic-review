# Fresh Search Expansion — Broad Sweep Addendum

A broader search identified five additional eligible workplace studies not present in the initial 23-record candidate set.

## Newly identified candidates

1. **Evaluation of a Voluntary Worksite Weight Loss Program on Metabolic Syndrome** (2015)
   - 3,880 participants from 93 companies.
   - Worksite behavioral weight-loss program.
   - Metabolic syndrome prevalence/outcomes assessed.
   - PMID: 26302220
   - DOI: 10.1089/met.2015.0075

2. **Effect of a Worksite-based Dietary Intervention Program for the Management of Metabolic Syndrome** (2016)
   - Male white-collar employees at a Seoul worksite.
   - Nutrition education/counselling plus workplace food-environment change.
   - Metabolic-syndrome components assessed.
   - Bibliographic DOI/details to verify from the journal record.

3. **Program Development and Effectiveness of Workplace Health Promotion Program for Preventing Metabolic Syndrome among Office Workers** (2017)
   - Office workers.
   - Workplace exercise, dietary planning, activity monitoring, and counselling.
   - Metabolic-syndrome prevention/risk-factor outcomes.
   - PMCID: PMC5580582

4. **Web-based high-intensity interval training with calorie restriction for workplace metabolic-syndrome** (2025)
   - Employees with workplace metabolic-syndrome risk.
   - Web-based HIIT plus calorie restriction.
   - Metabolic syndrome, cardiorespiratory fitness, and physical-activity outcomes.
   - DOI: 10.1093/occmed/kqaf079

5. **A healthy lifestyle education intervention for metabolic syndrome risk reduction among office workers in Ethiopia: a single-blind, randomised controlled trial** (2025)
   - Office workers in Ethiopia.
   - Lifestyle education targeting diet, physical activity, smoking, alcohol, and stress management.
   - Metabolic-syndrome risk factors assessed.
   - PMID: 41214020
   - DOI to verify.
