# Source-read extraction batch 200

Three outstanding occupational reports were checked against their original publisher records and converted into structured extraction rows.

## Added reports

1. **Tsai et al. 2025 — CHIEF military cohort (FC143-00982).** Among 2,854 military personnel free of baseline MetS, 662 incident MetS cases occurred over a median 5.8 years. Each one-unit higher BSRS-5 score was not associated with incident MetS (adjusted HR 1.006, 95% CI 0.975–1.039). The hypertension component showed a small positive association (HR 1.038, 95% CI 1.002–1.075). This report is flagged because BSRS-5 measures general mental stress rather than explicitly occupational/job stress; exposure-scope eligibility requires adjudication before synthesis.

2. **Saffari et al. 2025 — police SCT education RCT (FC143-00296).** The trial randomised 102 police officers. HDL increased in the intervention arm by 2.4 mg/dL (95% CI 1.9–3.0) versus 0.4 mg/dL (−1.4 to 2.2) in controls; ANCOVA p=0.035. Triglycerides decreased by 9.3 mg/dL (−13.7 to −4.9) versus a 1.5 mg/dL increase (−0.3 to 3.3) in controls; ANCOVA p<0.001. The source does not provide an adjusted treatment-effect CI for these rows, so none is inferred.

3. **Clemes et al. 2022 — SHIFT cluster RCT (FC143-00884).** The trial randomised 382 HGV drivers across 25 depots. Although activity and sitting outcomes improved at 6 months, the paper explicitly reports no between-group differences in anthropometric measures or cardiometabolic markers at 6 months. Attrition was substantial; 31.4% did not attend the 6-month assessment and the primary activity complete-case cohort contained 209 participants. Exact Table 4 cardiometabolic scalar estimates remain to be captured.
