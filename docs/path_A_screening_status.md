# Path A Screening Status — Locked Provisional Set

## Final decision on Gianturco et al. (2020)

**Exclude.** The HeRO-FiT study enrolled 100 outpatients from cardiovascular-prevention settings and randomized them to diet advice versus diet plus exercise. The study population was not defined by employment or workplace context.

## Provisional retained set

The strict workplace-focused re-screen therefore retains **4 studies**:

1. **Garbarino & Magnavita (2015)** — police officers; work stress; prospective metabolic syndrome outcome.
2. **Jovanović et al. (2020)** — security guards; occupational stress; metabolic/health outcomes.
3. **Aboma et al. (2020)** — working adults; metabolic syndrome prevalence and associated lifestyle factors.
4. **van Zon et al. (2020)** — 75,000 Dutch workers across occupational groups; metabolic syndrome prevalence/incidence and health behaviours.
