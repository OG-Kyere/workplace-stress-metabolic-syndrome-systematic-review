## Structured extraction added

**Songprasert et al. 2024 (FC143-00468)** — 104 intercity van drivers with uncontrolled hypertension were assigned to a PAOT intervention (n=52) or conventional programme (n=52). Mean systolic BP changed from 155.10±17.81 to 139.54±12.21 mmHg in the intervention group and from 158.46±15.83 to 152.17±14.39 mmHg in controls. The between-group repeated-measures ANOVA was significant (F=7.77, p=0.006). Diastolic BP was not significantly different between groups (F=2.63, p=0.108). At three months, BP control was reported in 38/52 intervention participants and 4/52 controls (OR 10.45, 95% CI 3.8–28.79). This is quasi-experimental rather than randomized evidence.
