# Search Strategy

## Source manuscript

The original thesis reports that the literature search was conducted in **February 2025** and covered English-language articles published from **2015 to 2025**.

### Databases reported

- PubMed/MEDLINE
- Scopus
- ScienceDirect

### Search concept reported in the thesis

```text
(Workplace stress OR Dietary Choices OR Physical Activity)
AND
(Metabolic Syndrome)
```

The thesis states that the same concept combination was applied across databases.

## Reproducibility gap

The archived thesis does **not** currently provide:

- complete database-specific search strings;
- field tags;
- subject headings (e.g., MeSH);
- exact search dates for each database;
- database result counts before deduplication;
- export format/details;
- deduplication procedure in sufficient detail.

These items must be reconstructed from the original search records, if available, before journal submission.

## Reported screening flow

The thesis reports:

- Records identified: **1,214**
- Unique records after removal of ineligible/duplicate records: **767**
- Records retained after title/abstract screening: **184**
- Full texts retrieved: **94**
- Full texts assessed: **90**
- Studies included: **27**

These figures should be reconciled against the PRISMA diagram and screening records before preprint submission.
