# Eligibility adjudication resolution 202

Three source-level eligibility flags were resolved against the prespecified review rule.

## FC143-00014 — Vivarelli et al. 2026
**Decision: retain.**

The report measures waist circumference directly and reports a significant day-versus-night-shift difference (85.7±9.9 vs 83.3±10.0 cm, p=0.025). Waist circumference is a prespecified central-adiposity component. The exposure is night-shift work, an occupational exposure.

The study should therefore remain eligible, but only its eligible MetS-component findings should enter synthesis. Novel indices such as METS-IR, TyG, WyG and IRCV must not be substituted for the prespecified MetS components.

## FC143-00150 — Di Prinzio et al. 2025
**Decision: retain.**

This is a workplace dietary intervention among healthcare workers. It measures several prespecified MetS components, including triglycerides, HDL cholesterol, systolic and diastolic blood pressure, fasting glucose and abdominal circumference. The fact that it also reports BMI, WHR, HbA1c, LDL and other outcomes does not invalidate the eligible component outcomes.

Only prespecified MetS-component results should contribute to the review synthesis.

## FC143-00982 — Tsai et al. 2025 CHIEF
**Decision: exclude from the primary synthesis.**

The exposure is general mental stress assessed with BSRS-5, covering depression, anxiety, hostility, insomnia and interpersonal sensitivity. It is not an occupational/job-stress measure. The fact that participants are military personnel does not make a general psychological-distress exposure work-related.

Physical activity and aerobic fitness are included as covariates rather than the primary exposure. The report therefore fails the prespecified exposure/intervention criterion and should be coded as a full-text exclusion for wrong exposure/intervention.

## Consequence

The provisional primary inclusion count should decrease by one when the master screening ledger is next regenerated. The current manuscript counts remain provisional until the central screening files are updated and reconciled.
