# Eligibility Criteria

## Criteria reported in the thesis

### Population
Adults of working age, generally 18–65 years, employed or working in workplace settings, with or without diagnosed metabolic syndrome.

### Concept
Workplace/occupational stress, dietary factors, and/or physical activity in relation to metabolic syndrome or related metabolic risk.

### Context
Workplace and everyday-life settings relevant to occupational and lifestyle exposures.

### Language and publication type
The thesis reports inclusion of English-language, peer-reviewed original research and exclusion of editorials, conference abstracts, study protocols, and grey literature.

## Internal inconsistency requiring resolution

Two parts of Chapter 3 conflict:

1. The general eligibility section allows **qualitative, quantitative, and mixed-method studies**.
2. The later study-selection section says primary studies had to be **randomized controlled trials**.

However, the final included-study list contains randomized trials, observational studies, prospective studies, prevalence studies, and at least one protocol.

The journal manuscript must therefore define one coherent set of eligibility criteria and apply it consistently to all 27 candidate studies.
