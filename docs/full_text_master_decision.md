# Full-Text Screening Master Decision

## Current status

After the fresh workplace-focused search and verification sweep, the evidence base contains **28 completed original studies that meet the working inclusion criteria at full-text/record level**.

These studies span:
- occupational/work stress;
- physical activity and sedentary behaviour;
- workplace exercise interventions;
- dietary quality and worksite dietary interventions;
- multicomponent workplace lifestyle programmes;
- metabolic syndrome prevalence, incidence, severity, and recognized MetS components.

## Included-study status

All 28 records in `data/verified_include_candidates.csv` are currently retained for the revised review.

This does **not** mean risk of bias is low; inclusion and methodological quality are separate decisions.

## Companion / overlapping reports

### Dutch Lifelines cohort
- van Zon et al. (2020)
- Runge et al. (2021)

Both are retained as distinct reports from the same underlying cohort. They will not be treated as independent populations in any participant-total statement.

### Ethiopian lifestyle-intervention programme
- The 2024 protocol is excluded.
- The completed 2025 randomized trial by Bogale et al. is included.
- The 2026 feasibility/acceptability paper is a companion process-evaluation report and should not be counted as a separate effectiveness study unless used specifically for implementation context.

## Next stage

The study set is now ready for design classification and risk-of-bias/quality appraisal.

The final PRISMA diagram will require documented database search counts and deduplication totals from the fresh search; the number 28 is the current included-study total, not the total number of records identified.
