# Journal-target strategy status

## Preferred target: Occupational and Environmental Medicine (BMJ)

Checked against current journal guidance on 6 October 2026.

### Why it is a stronger fit

Occupational and Environmental Medicine explicitly welcomes **systematic reviews and meta-analyses** with:
- a well-defined hypothesis/question;
- explicit methodology;
- quality assessment;
- occupational or environmental health relevance.

The journal also states that systematic reviews should follow **PRISMA** guidance.

### Current limits

For systematic review and meta-analysis articles:
- word count: up to 4,500;
- tables/illustrations: up to 5;
- references: up to 60;
- key-message box required after the abstract.

The current manuscript fits these limits:
- main text is well below 4,500 words;
- 34 active-study references;
- current display items = 4, with PRISMA expected to become the fifth.

### Publication-cost model

OEM uses a hybrid subscription model.

Authors may choose:
- a standard subscription publication route with **no publication charge**; or
- optional open access for an APC.

This satisfies the project's preference to avoid mandatory publication fees.

### Current recommendation

**Promote OEM to preferred target.**

Do not alter the scientific manuscript until the PRISMA counts are complete.

After PRISMA is frozen, retarget formatting by:
1. replacing Occupational Medicine-specific front matter;
2. adding OEM key messages:
   - What is already known on this topic;
   - What this study adds;
   - How this study might affect research, practice or policy;
3. confirming OEM abstract structure at submission;
4. keeping total display items at five or fewer;
5. updating the cover letter and title-page metadata;
6. preserving the PRISMA flow and JBI appraisal.

## Previous target: Occupational Medicine (OUP)

Status: **secondary/conditional target**.

Reason:
The current author guidance describes its review format as qualitative rather than PRISMA-style, creating an article-type mismatch with the present manuscript.

## Alternative: Annals of Work Exposures and Health (OUP)

Status: **credible backup**.

Strengths:
- explicitly accepts review articles with a methodology for evidence gathering;
- recommends PRISMA best practices;
- permits narrative or quantitative synthesis;
- up to 5,000 words and six tables/figures;
- standard subscription licence has no licence charge, page charge, or mandatory APC.

Potential limitation:
- journal scope places greater emphasis on occupational exposures/exposure science than the present broader lifestyle/intervention review.
