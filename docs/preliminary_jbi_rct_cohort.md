# Preliminary JBI Appraisal — RCTs and Cohorts

## Scope

This file documents the first-pass JBI appraisal of the 6 randomized trials and 6 cohort/longitudinal studies in the current included set.

Detailed item-level tables are stored in:

- `data/jbi_rct_preliminary.csv`
- `data/jbi_cohort_preliminary.csv`

## Important caution

These are **preliminary**, source-supported judgments.

Items are marked **Unclear** when the accessible publication record did not clearly establish the methodological feature. No item was inferred simply because a study was published in a high-impact journal or described as randomized/prospective.

## RCT observations

### Haufe et al. (2019)
The trial is clearly randomized, parallel-group, and assessor-blind. Exercise participants and intervention deliverers could not realistically be blinded. Allocation concealment, attrition handling, and analysis-by-assigned-group require confirmation from the full methods/report.

### Ma et al. (2025)
The study is described as randomized, but the intervention is organized around two canteens. The full paper must be checked carefully to determine the true unit of randomization, allocation concealment, and whether any cluster structure was handled analytically.

### Bogale et al. (2025)
The completed Ethiopian trial is cluster-randomized and single-masked. The masking target, concealment method, and missing-data/ITT details still require explicit confirmation.

## Cohort observations

The six cohort/longitudinal studies generally have strong structural features:
- clearly defined worker populations;
- prospective outcome ascertainment;
- explicit confounder adjustment;
- objective or standardized MetS definitions;
- adequate follow-up periods.

The main unresolved domains are usually:
- completeness of follow-up;
- handling of attrition/missing data.

## No summary score yet

No study has been assigned a numeric total or a final 'low/moderate/high risk' label at this stage.

The final appraisal will be locked only after the remaining full-method details are verified.
