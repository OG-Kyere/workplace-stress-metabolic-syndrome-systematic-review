# Evidence Synthesis Framework

## Purpose

The revised Results will be organized by **exposure/intervention domain**, not by superficial characteristics such as publication year or country counts.

This structure better answers the review question and prevents descriptive study metadata from overshadowing the substantive evidence.

## Domain 1 — Occupational and work-related stress

Priority evidence:
- Garbarino & Magnavita (2015)
- Yamaguchi et al. (2018)
- Jovanović et al. (2020)
- Eftekhari et al. (2021)
- Zhang et al. (2024)
- Ayeltigah et al. (2026, healthcare professionals)
- Ayeltigah et al. (2026, university workers)
- Ayeltigah et al. (2026, military personnel)

### Synthesis questions
- Is higher occupational stress associated with greater MetS prevalence or incidence?
- Which MetS components appear most consistently associated with work stress?
- Do prospective findings support the cross-sectional pattern?
- Which occupational groups appear most vulnerable?

## Domain 2 — Diet and workplace nutrition

Priority evidence:
- Appiah et al. (2020)
- Apprey et al. (2022)
- Sharifi et al. (2025)
- Ma et al. (2025)
- Kim et al. (2016)
- dos Reis et al. (2026)
- Petek & Karabudak (2026)
- Reinoso-Barbero et al. (2026)
- Ayeltigah et al. (2026 studies)

### Synthesis questions
- Which dietary patterns are associated with MetS or component risk?
- Do worksite dietary interventions improve MetS outcomes?
- Are associations consistent across occupational settings?
- How much evidence is intervention-based versus observational?

## Domain 3 — Physical activity and sedentary behaviour

Priority evidence:
- Kuwahara et al. (2016)
- Ko et al. (2016)
- Sedentary occupation workers study (2017)
- Haufe et al. (2019)
- Motuma/Aboma et al. (2020)
- Runge et al. (2021)
- So et al. (2025)
- Ayeltigah et al. (2026 studies)

### Synthesis questions
- Is leisure-time activity more protective than occupational activity?
- Does sedentary work increase MetS risk independently of leisure activity?
- What do exercise interventions show about modifiability of risk?
- Are objective and self-reported activity measures directionally consistent?

## Domain 4 — Multicomponent workplace lifestyle interventions

Priority evidence:
- Earnest & Church (2015)
- Ryu et al. (2017)
- Haufe et al. (2019)
- Ma et al. (2025)
- So et al. (2025)
- Bogale et al. (2025)

### Synthesis questions
- Do workplace programmes reduce MetS severity or individual components?
- Which combination of diet, physical activity, self-monitoring, and counselling appears most promising?
- Are benefits sustained beyond the intervention period?
- How much causal confidence is supported by randomized versus uncontrolled designs?

## Evidence weighting

When studies disagree, interpretation will prioritize:
1. randomized trials;
2. prospective cohort studies;
3. quasi-experimental intervention studies;
4. analytical cross-sectional studies.

Large sample size alone will not override major design limitations.

## Planned Results structure

1. Study selection and characteristics
2. Risk of bias / methodological quality
3. Occupational stress and MetS
4. Diet and MetS
5. Physical activity/sedentary behaviour and MetS
6. Multicomponent workplace interventions
7. Summary of consistency, heterogeneity, and evidence gaps

## Planned Discussion structure

The Discussion will distinguish:
- evidence of association;
- evidence of temporal relationship;
- evidence from intervention;
- remaining uncertainty.

No causal language will be used for cross-sectional associations.
