# Effect-estimate verification policy

The manuscript contains both source-verified and still-pending quantitative estimates.

## Source-verified in the current pass
- Garbarino & Magnavita (2015): adjusted OR 2.68 for incident MetS.
- Yamaguchi et al. (2018): adjusted OR 3.27 for low-to-high job demands.
- Kuwahara et al. (2016): HR 0.74 and 0.81 for mixed leisure exercise categories.
- Motuma et al. (2020): APR 2.29 for prolonged sedentary behaviour and APR 0.04 for high fruit/vegetable intake.

## Principle
An estimate is marked "Verified against source record" only when the underlying source text was directly checked. Bibliographic verification alone is not treated as effect-size verification.
