# Source-read clinical extraction batch 206

Three additional eligible reports were checked against original publication sources.

## Bartosiewicz et al. 2021 — Polish nurses (FC143-00650)

Among 108 professionally active nurses, high physical activity on the IPAQ was the only significant multivariable activity predictor of elevated blood pressure among the examined MetS components. Relative to insufficient activity, high activity had OR **0.245** for blood pressure >130/85 mmHg (source marks p<0.05). The published table does not provide a confidence interval, so none is inferred.

## Tsou et al. 2021 — Taiwanese tertiary-hospital nurses (FC143-00652)

Among 1,758 nurses, MetS prevalence was 13.84%. Occupational burnout was associated with MetS with OR **1.70** (95% CI **1.04-3.05**). Because the design is cross-sectional, this is retained as an association rather than an incident-risk estimate.

## Gómez-Recasens et al. 2023 — Spanish environmental-services employees (FC143-00910)

Among 751 employees, moderate-high occupational physical activity was associated with lower waist circumference after adjustment for age, sex, alcohol consumption and global physical activity: beta **-2.438 cm** (95% CI **-4.39 to -0.48**, p=0.015).
