# Fresh Search Protocol — Path A

## Rationale

The original screening spreadsheet is unavailable, and a fresh search identified clearly eligible workplace/metabolic-syndrome studies that were absent from the thesis's 27-study appendix.

Accordingly, the journal/preprint version will use a **new, reproducible literature search** rather than treating the archived thesis screening as complete.

## Review scope

Working adults / occupational populations, with:
- workplace or occupational stress;
- diet, dietary pattern, nutrition, or workplace food intervention;
- physical activity, exercise, sedentary behaviour, or work-related activity;

and outcomes including:
- metabolic syndrome;
- metabolic-syndrome incidence/prevalence;
- recognized metabolic-syndrome components or cardiometabolic biomarkers when explicitly linked to MetS.

## Date range

2015 through 2026.

Because the manuscript is being revised in 2026, the search is being updated through the current year rather than artificially stopping at 2025.

## Core PubMed concept

```text
("metabolic syndrome"[Title/Abstract] OR "Metabolic Syndrome"[MeSH Terms])
AND
(worker*[Title/Abstract] OR employee*[Title/Abstract] OR workplace[Title/Abstract]
 OR occupation*[Title/Abstract] OR "shift work"[Title/Abstract]
 OR "white-collar"[Title/Abstract] OR "blue-collar"[Title/Abstract]
 OR police[Title/Abstract] OR driver*[Title/Abstract]
 OR healthcare[Title/Abstract])
AND
(stress[Title/Abstract] OR "job strain"[Title/Abstract]
 OR "physical activity"[Title/Abstract] OR exercise[Title/Abstract]
 OR sedentary[Title/Abstract] OR diet*[Title/Abstract]
 OR nutrition*[Title/Abstract])
AND
("2015"[Date - Publication] : "2026"[Date - Publication])
```

## Supplementary search strategy

Separate exposure-focused searches will also be run for:

1. occupational/work stress + metabolic syndrome;
2. physical activity/exercise/sedentary work + metabolic syndrome;
3. diet/nutrition/worksite food intervention + metabolic syndrome;
4. shift work + metabolic syndrome;
5. occupation/worker/employee + metabolic syndrome.

## Databases / sources

Primary:
- PubMed/MEDLINE

Supplementary discovery:
- references of relevant systematic reviews/meta-analyses;
- forward/backward citation checking;
- journal and DOI verification through publisher/index records;
- additional bibliographic databases if accessible.

## Important limitation

A web search alone is not treated as a formal database export. The final PRISMA counts must be based on documented search outputs and deduplication.

## Screening rule

Two-stage screening:
1. title/abstract;
2. full text.

Studies must be completed original research in employed/occupational populations and must report MetS or directly relevant cardiometabolic outcomes.
