# OEM current submission requirements

Verified against the current Occupational and Environmental Medicine author guidance on 6 October 2026.

## Systematic review and meta-analysis article type

OEM welcomes systematic reviews with:
- a well-defined question or hypothesis;
- explicit methodology;
- quality assessment;
- relevance to occupational or environmental health.

Systematic reviews should follow **PRISMA** guidance.

## Limits

- Main text: **up to 4,500 words**
- Tables/illustrations: **up to 5**
- References: **up to 60**

## Key messages

A summary box is required after the abstract under:
- What is already known on this topic
- What this study adds
- How this study might affect research, practice or policy

The key-message box should be concise and distinct from the abstract.

## Peer review

OEM uses **single-anonymised peer review**.

## Statistical reporting

OEM prefers:
- measures of association with confidence intervals;
- exact quantitative P values when P values are reported, where available;
- restrained numerical precision.

Systematic reviews should follow PRISMA.

## Data availability

OEM follows BMJ Tier 3 data policy and requires a Data Availability Statement for research articles.

## ORCID

An ORCID iD is mandatory for the submitting author.

## Publication charges

Open access is optional and carries an APC. The journal also offers the standard non-open-access publication route; there are no submission, page or online-only colour-figure charges.
