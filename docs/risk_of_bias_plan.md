# Risk-of-Bias / Quality Appraisal Plan

The thesis contains a general "assessment of trustworthiness" section but does not document a study-level critical appraisal using a validated design-specific tool.

Because the included studies appear to span multiple designs, the journal version should use design-appropriate appraisal methods rather than one generic checklist.

## Proposed workflow

1. Confirm the final eligible study set.
2. Classify each study by design.
3. Apply an appropriate validated appraisal tool for each design.
4. Perform appraisal independently by at least two reviewers where feasible.
5. Resolve disagreements by discussion or a third reviewer.
6. Report study-level judgments transparently in a supplementary table.
7. Use risk-of-bias findings when interpreting the certainty and limitations of the evidence.

## Important

No risk-of-bias ratings should be invented retrospectively from titles or abstracts alone. Ratings must be based on the full reports.
